The stars are the work of God's fingers:

!!! note "Psalm 8:3"

    When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers,  the moon and the stars which you set in place—

But salvation is the work of his right hand:

!!! note "Psalm 98:1"

    Sing to Yahweh a new song, for he has done wonders.  His right hand and his holy arm have secured his victory.

In Genesis 1, God created the Heavens and the earth. He brings `order to chaos`.

## How can we bring order to chaos?

- cleaning the playroom

## How can we rest?

- enjoy the creation God has given us
- rejoice in salvation, rejoicing brings refreshment to the soul
